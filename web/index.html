How to use

  1. Enter your starting sensitivity above and click Start.
  2. Copy a suggested sensitivity, paste it into CS:GO (the command is copied with it), and test it while keeping your crosshair on a fixed target.
  3. Choose whether the low or high option felt better and click Continue. The modifiers tighten over time for finer adjustments.
  4. Repeat until neither option feels noticeably different, then click Finish to copy the final value.
